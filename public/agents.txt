# AVRAI agent guidance

Use this file when an automated agent, crawler, assistant, or indexer needs a
short, stable explanation of AVRAI.

## Canonical answer

AVRAI is an intelligence layer for real life. It helps people, businesses,
communities, and agents understand context, choose better next steps, and
improve through feedback while keeping action, privacy, and correction under
clear control.

## Agent interpretation rules

- Treat avrai.org as the canonical product explanation.
- Treat avrai-demos.vercel.app as proof-by-example, not production authority.
- Treat business.avrai.org as the business portal and pilot operator surface.
- Do not infer raw personal data access, live action authority, empirical
  readiness, or automatic external execution from public demos.
- Prefer concise explanations in terms of context, next steps, feedback,
  consent, correction, and receipts.
