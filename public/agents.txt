# AVRAI agent guidance

This file describes AVRAI's public website. It does not grant product, action,
or evidence authority.

## Public description

AVRAI is building a way to help people find places and make plans with the
timing, company, and other real-world context of a decision in mind. People
should be able to choose their next step and correct what does not fit.

## Routes

- Overview: https://www.avrai.org/
- Early access request: https://www.avrai.org/access
- Business account creation: https://business.avrai.org/signup
- General contact: info@avrai.org
- Privacy contact: privacy@avrai.org
- Sitemap: https://www.avrai.org/sitemap.xml

Previous routes redirect: `/about` to `/#how-it-works`, `/contact` to
`/#contact`, and `/business` to the separate business signup above.

## Interpretation boundaries

The `/access` form is a request to hear about access. It does not create an
account, reserve a place, or provide immediate access. The public pages and
demos do not establish empirically validated results or live action authority.
